## Prompts de producción

**Dino e invitación.** Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished original 3D illustration for Diverdino, a children's birthday and family celebration venue in Elche. Portrait 4:5 composition, friendly smiling lime green baby brontosaurus mascot with soft orange small back spikes and creamy yellow belly, shiny but tasteful toy/clay material, large expressive eyes. Dinosaur holds a cream birthday invitation sealed with a tiny fuchsia ribbon. Curving turquoise balloon arch, a few fuchsia and warm yellow balloons, small pastel birthday cake on a low plinth, colorful sparse confetti. Sophisticated playful editorial composition, generous breathing space, soft warm daylight and subtle shadows, off-white and turquoise sculptural backdrop. Exact brand palette turquoise #4fc3dc, fuchsia #e51b83, green #56b827, yellow #fbc000, orange #f08219. Rich beautiful tactile materials, professionally art directed, magical and welcoming for families, exceptional quality. Image only, NO text, NO letters, NO watermark, NO logos, NO human faces, NO real venue interior, no invented installation. Compose mascot centrally with clear silhouette and head safely inside crop.

**Familia y amigos.** Use case: stylized-concept. Original premium 3D toy/clay illustration for Diverdino's family celebrations. Landscape 4:3. Art direction: warm off-white sculptural studio with turquoise #4fc3dc, fuchsia #e51b83, friendly green #56b827, yellow #fbc000 and orange #f08219. A beautiful circular table with a small festive cake, charming mismatched colorful miniature chairs, an open cream invitation with fuchsia satin bow, a handful of floating balloons and playful paper confetti. A small smiling lime green baby brontosaurus toy mascot with yellow belly and small orange back spikes perched beside table, coherent high-end child friendly world. Welcoming family get-together without people; stylish simple objects, soft realistic material detail, thoughtful composition and indirect sunlight, clean spacious background. An illustration, not an architectural image or photograph of a real venue. No text, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no alcohol.

**Karaoke y celebraciones.** Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original refined, playful 3D illustration for Diverdino's 16th and 18th birthday celebrations. Landscape 4:3 composition. Sculptural fuchsia #e51b83 and warm white studio backdrop, turquoise #4fc3dc plinths, green #56b827 and yellow #fbc000 accents. A large satin turquoise ribbon encircles a cream invitation, a stylish wireless karaoke microphone leaning on a sculptural gift box, tasteful green/fuchsia/yellow balloons, small birthday cake and sparse suspended confetti. Sophisticated celebratory youthful scene that still belongs to a family venue, soft studio lighting, tactile clay and satin surfaces, art directed editorial still life. Clean calm composition with delightful details, no nightclub, no people, no alcohol, no text, no numbers, no logos or watermarks. Not a photograph of a real venue. High quality and visual consistency with a friendly dinosaur birthday brand.
